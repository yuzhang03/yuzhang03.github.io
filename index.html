Research Interests.

My lab studies how AI systems can make reliable decisions when information is missing or conditions change. In robotics and geospatial AI, we ask what information to gather next, how to update predictions, and when there is enough information to act.

  1. 3D World Models and Active Mapping
    We study how robots build 3D maps when they can see only part of their surroundings. We ask how predictions about unseen areas guide where robots look and move next, and how robots can update those predictions as they gather more information.
  2. Active Multimodal 3D Perception for Robotic Manipulation
    We study how robots use sight and touch together to understand an object's shape and tell when and how they make contact with it. We ask how robots can decide what to look at or touch next and adapt to changing conditions while keeping what they have already learned.
  3. Geospatial AI
    We build AI models that bring together data from different sources to understand places and environments, from broad regional patterns to local details. We study how well these models work in new places and over time, and how much confidence to place in their predictions when data are missing or collected in different ways.
